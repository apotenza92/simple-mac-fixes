- In window previews in the Dock, the window under the pointer now plays live, so you can see a video or anything else that moves before you pick it. The other windows keep their picture, so the preview stays fast.
- When you rest on a window in the preview, the window that shows in its place plays live too.
- Window names now start next to the window's buttons. Long names fade out at their edges as they slide, and slide back when you move to another window.
- The preview now fades out as it closes, like the Dock's own menus. If you turned on Reduce Motion in your Mac's settings, or Instant Dock, it opens and closes at once.
- Settings now has tabs along the top, so each kind of fix has its own page.
- A new Reset Settings button puts every switch back to how it was when you first installed the app.
- Settings explains each part in a line under its name, and Instant Dock and instant space switching now say clearly that they turn off animations.
