- Maximized windows now grow and shrink with the same smooth movement and speed as the system's own fill.
- Dragging a maximized window by its title bar now works like the system's own: the window stays still for a moment, then comes away under the pointer at its earlier size and follows it.
- New choice under "Maximize windows" in Settings: leave a gap between a maximized window and the edges of the screen (off, small, medium or large).
- Double-clicking the title bar now maximizes windows in more apps, such as T3 Code and GitHub Desktop, instead of filling the screen the system's way.
- Clicking the green button no longer sometimes opens full screen when another app is slow to answer.
- Dragging a maximized Mail window now restores it, instead of moving it at its maximized size.
- The Windows tab in Settings now fits its content, so there is nothing to scroll.
