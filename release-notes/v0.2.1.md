- The pointer no longer stops or jumps when it is over a Dock app that shows window previews.
- Clicking near the top of a Dock app now brings the app forward while its window previews show.
- Minimizing a window from its preview keeps a clear picture of it, and the preview stays open.
- Minimized windows and windows of a hidden app now say "Minimized" or "Hidden", and they no longer appear full size on the screen when you point at them.
- The Dock no longer shows an app's name under its window previews while you scroll on the app to pick a window.
- Mouse wheel scrolling no longer waits while the app is busy.
