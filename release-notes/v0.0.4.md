- New: Instant desktop switching. Your shortcuts for moving between desktops switch straight away, without the slide. Press Control-Option-Command and a number to jump to that desktop, or Control-Option-Command-Tab and -~ to go round them all.
- New: Instant Dock. When the Dock is hidden, it appears as soon as you point at it. Your Dock settings go back to normal when Simple Mac Fixes quits.
- Clicks no longer get stuck: clicking apps in the Dock or windows elsewhere stays responsive even when an app is busy.
