- Window previews open straight away and stay up while you move up to them, even if you pass over other apps on the way.
- Windows are shown most recent first, and the first one is picked for you. Click the app in the Dock to go to it, or scroll on the app to move between windows and bring each one forward.
- Rest on a preview to see that window at its real size and place before you pick it.
- Previews use the same glass as the rest of your Mac, and their window buttons look and behave like the real ones. The green button maximizes or goes full screen, the same as on the window itself.
- Previews update after you minimize or maximize from them, and long window names stay inside their preview.
- With the Dock on the side, previews are listed top to bottom.
- Maximize windows works on normal window buttons again, and dragging a maximized window puts it back to its old size, even after the app restarts.
- Maximize fits the window to the right screen when you have more than one display.
- New: Space shortcuts are now their own switch, listed under Instant space switching with every shortcut shown.
- Instant Dock, Instant space switching and Space shortcuts start off for new users. If you already use them, they stay on.
