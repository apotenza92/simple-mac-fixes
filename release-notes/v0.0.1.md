First release of Simple Mac Fixes: one menu-bar app with a switch for each fix.

- Cut and paste files in Finder with ⌘X and ⌘V.
- Traditional scrolling for wheel mice; trackpads and the Magic Mouse keep your System Settings direction.
- Maximize windows with the green button or a title-bar double-click, drag the title bar to restore, and Option-click the green button for full screen.
- Click a Dock app with more than one window to see them in App Exposé; Option-click a Dock folder to open it in Finder.
- Volume and brightness keys for audio interfaces, USB DACs and external monitors.

Replaces Macsimize, Dockmint and Simple Mac Keyboard Control, which quit automatically while Simple Mac Fixes runs.
