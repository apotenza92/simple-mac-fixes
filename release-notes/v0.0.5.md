- New: Window previews in the Dock. Point at an app with more than one window to see them all, even minimized and hidden ones and windows on other spaces. Click one to go to it, or close, minimize or maximize it from the preview.
- The app's name stays up above the preview, just like the Dock shows it.
- If your Dock hides itself, it stays up while a preview is open.
- Clicking an app in the Dock no longer opens the windows view. Window previews replace it.
- Option-clicking Dock folders keeps working after the Dock restarts.
