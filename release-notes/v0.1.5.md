- Window previews in the Dock have a new look that matches the Dock. Each window is a larger card with its picture and name, on the same glass as the Dock.
- Each card now has the window's own close, minimize and maximize buttons next to its name, always in the same place.
- Window names are easier to read on any desktop picture, in light and dark mode.
- The preview now covers the app's name under the pointer, and it stays in line with the icon when the Dock grows the icons.
- When an app has more windows than fit on the screen, you can scroll through them.
- The preview opens faster, opens at once with no fade, and no longer flashes the first time you pick a window.
- Window names now show correctly for browsers that give every window the app's name.
