- Window previews in the Dock open and change faster and more smoothly as you move along the Dock.
- The Dock no longer shows another app's name under a preview, also when you move quickly between apps or click an icon.
- Window pictures are ready when a preview opens, so you no longer see the app's icon for a moment first.
- Pointing at an app shows its window in place straight away, and it plays live, like the window under the pointer.
- Click the app's icon to close the preview and go to the window you picked. You can stay on the icon, scroll to a window and click.
- Scrolling on an app's icon now goes round its windows. It stops at the first and last window; scroll again after a short pause to go round.
- Keys you type while a preview is open now reach the app you are using.
- A preview no longer stays on the wrong app or closes by itself when you move slowly along the Dock.
- Window pictures sit in the middle of their cards.
